STC GAMEHUB

STC Gamehub is a creative platform dedicated to producing immersive Concert Audio Effects and Minecraft Music Video (MV) Covers. The brand name reflects its two core content pillars:

  • StudioConcerts (STC) represents concert audio production, while GameHub focuses on Minecraft music video creations.
  • STC GameHub was not the original title of the channel. When it was launched on May 16, 2022, it was named FU123, with content initially focused on Minecraft building projects.
  • Over time, the channel went through several rebranding phases FU123, FUGaming, FUGamingCraft, Zuki, and StudioConcerts before officially adopting STC GameHub, a name that perfectly captures the vision and identity of the channel. Community &
