Introduction

November 28, 2025

STC Gamehub is a creative platform dedicated to producing immersive Concert Audio Effects and Minecraft Music Video (MV) Covers. The brand name reflects its two core content pillars: StudioConcerts (STC) represents concert audio production, while GameHub focuses on Minecraft music video creations. STC GameHub was not the original title of the channel. When it was launched on May 16, 2022, it was named FU123, with content initially focused on Minecraft building projects. Over time, the channel went through several rebranding phases FU123, FUGaming, FUGamingCraft, Zuki, and StudioConcerts before officially adopting STC GameHub, a name that perfectly captures the vision and identity of the channel.


Channel Evolution: From FUGamingCraft to Audio Production

My journey into music focused content began when my cousins introduced me to BLACKPINK’s 2020 release, THE ALBUM, specifically the music video for "Lovesick Girls." Inspired by their music and impressed by their 75-million-subscriber milestone, I developed a strong interest in their work.


On July 31, 2022, I rebranded the channel to FUGaming (and later FUGamingCraft) to focus on BLACKPINK inspired Minecraft music video parodies. Early in this phase, I made an error in judgment by re-uploading a "How You Like That" Minecraft MV cover created by another artist without obtaining permission. After receiving feedback from viewers highlighting the issue, I felt deeply remorseful. Although it was never my intention to misuse someone else's work, I immediately deleted the video and committed to producing 100% original content moving forward.


My first original release, the "Pink Venom" Minecraft MV Cover, became a significant milestone, achieving thousands of views for the first time on the channel. Following this success, I produced a cover for "Ready for Love" and later collaborated with creator +ZaaZu+, obtaining explicit permission to feature their work on the channel.


On November 25, 2022, I began experimenting with Concert Audio Effects. Although my early edits lacked refined frequency balance, I continually adjusted and improved my technique. A major breakthrough occurred on November 28, 2023, following the debut of YG Entertainment's group BABYMONSTER. My concert effect edit for "Batter Up" gained significant traction, becoming the most popular video on the channel at the time. The overwhelmingly positive community feedback encouraged me to continue creating and fulfilling subscriber requests, despite managing routine copyright claims.


StudioConcerts & The Future of STC GameHub

Growth continued into early 2024. On April 1, 2024, I released a concert audio effect for BABYMONSTER's "SHEESH," which reached 2,000 views. On April 19, 2024, a compilation featuring concert edits of "SHEESH," "Batter Up," and "Stuck In The Middle" surged in popularity, achieving over 79,000 views the second highest view count in the channel's history.


In 2025, I recently uploaded a concert effect edit for "Like JENNIE." Now fully operating under the STC GameHub brand, I plan to continue audio production while resuming Minecraft MV cover projects as soon as possible. Thank you for your continued support!
